Notes for social posts

  • Three background choices that stay on-brand: white, #f7f7f5 off-white, or #141414 near-black. Green as a full-bleed background works, but only with white type.
  • Green is an accent, not a wash. In the app it covers roughly one element per screen — a button, a number, one italic word.
  • Headlines are Fraunces 600 with tight negative tracking; the italic-green treatment marks one or two words, never a whole line.
  • Never place body text on the green tint #E1F5EE in anything lighter than #0F6E56.
  • Icons keep 2px stroke at any size — scale the viewBox, not the stroke.
  • The WhatsApp colours belong to chat screenshots only; using them elsewhere reads as someone else's brand.
